## The answer in one paragraph
Energy will not replace the dollar you spend. It is replacing gold's old job: the scarce physical thing that caps how much real output any money can claim. In an AI and robot economy output scales with delivered power, and power cannot be printed. Energy is also entering the system as collateral inside the dollar (power plants, data centers, PPAs and GPUs backing dollar loans, compute futures settling in dollars), a "watt-dollar" descended from the 1974 petrodollar. It fails as spendable money because it is perishable, location-bound and getting cheaper. Musk's "currency measured in wattage and tonnage" is a post-scarcity accounting idea close to the 1930s Technocracy plan, and its real problem is who allocates. Confidence 65 (Plausible).

## How the Fed and Treasury are tackling the debt
They are managing who must buy it, not shrinking it. Captive demand is the chosen strategy: stablecoin reserves forced into short bills from 2027-01-18, bank leverage relief, bills sized to money funds, long-bond buybacks, repo lines so Japan borrows instead of selling. The Fed under Warsh is hiking. Inflation above the 3.5% average debt cost erodes the ratio quietly. No gold revaluation, no Bitcoin buying, no formal accord. Set 1 (gold, bonds, credit) is absorbing Set 2 (stablecoins, crypto rails): an acquisition, not a merger.
