*The rivals' strongest card is hardware, not currency. The shocks that matter in 2026 came from oil, the yen and Paris.*

A popular story says rivals will dethrone the dollar with a BRICS currency or a Treasury fire sale. The project scored every lever with one formula (cost to the US, times likelihood, times how cheap the move is for the rival, divided by 25) and found the money levers weak. BRICS agreed on no currency in New Delhi in September 2026 (score 0.12). A Treasury fire sale would hurt China most (0.08). mBridge has handled about $55 billion in total (0.60).

### The battery swap

Electric freight moves goods prices away from dollar oil, but into a different chokepoint. China makes over 80% of battery cells, about 85% of cathode and more than 90% of anode material. On a concentration index, battery mid-stream stages score about 5,250 to 9,400, against oil's 830 to 906 by country. But oil is a flow and a battery is a stock: cut off oil and trucks stop within weeks, cut off batteries and only new vehicles and the 8% to 12% of packs replaced each year are exposed (R3-12, Plausible, 70). The country electrifying fastest is China itself: new-energy heavy trucks were 38.1% of sales in January to September 2026, a hedge against its own Hormuz exposure.

### The 2026 shocks

- Hormuz. A war closed the strait in practice. Brent reached $115.22 and US diesel $6.276 a gallon, up 73% in a year. War-risk insurance jumped to 3.5% to 7.5% of hull value per transit from 0.10% to 0.25%. Markets give normal traffic before 2028 about 48%. The freight spike adds roughly 1 point to CPI, peaking about a year later.
- The yen. USD/JPY neared 164 in July. Japan spent ¥27.1 trillion defending it in 2026 and the Bank of Japan hiked on 18 September. Round 5 update: Japanese yields rose about 85bp this year and account for about 17bp to 18bp of the rise in US real yields. Today it is 157.10, below the 160 to 164 intervention zone.
- France. The France to Germany spread went from 87 to 157.5 basis points between 10 September and 21 October. Tripwires are 175 (ECB adjustments) and 200 (its TPI backstop). Markets put a Le Pen presidency in 2027 at about 46. Moody's decision on France's Aa3 negative rating was due after the close on 23 October: thin markets said 87.5% for a downgrade by 31 October, the court about 70. Round 5 update: Moody's held Aa3 negative on 23 October, conditional on a 2027 budget, so the desk's 70 is a pending score that likely beats the market.

> When the dollar is strong, the US exports inflation. In 2022 the IMF estimated a 10% dollar rise passes about 1 point of inflation to other countries. In 2026 the absorbers were oil importers with weak currencies: the rupee near 96.9, the Philippine peso past 60, the rupiah at 17,315. But the US was not insulated either: energy CPI rose 20.4%. The court's conclusion: the US can export inflation or weaken the dollar to help industry, not both at once.

### Round 4 update: could a rival inherit?

Round 4 tested succession directly. The dollar's share of allocated reserves fell from about 71% in 2000 to 56.70%, but the yuan holds only 2.11%. The lost share went to gold and a crowd of smaller currencies. China as it is cannot inherit, because a reserve currency must let money leave freely. If it opened its capital account, the court puts about 60% on money first running into dollars, then 9 to 18 years to reach even 10% of reserves. In the US-made shock of early 2025, the market's emergency heirs were the euro, the yen and gold, not the yuan. The desk's odds of any single currency overtaking the dollar are under 1% to 2028, 1% to 2035 and 5% to 2050. Chapter 13 tells the full story.

**Check yourself.** Q: Why is a solar know-how ban more dangerous to the US than a BRICS currency?
A: Because the watt-dollar needs energy hardware that the US cannot print. A rival currency needs others to trust and hold it, which has not happened. Blocking the factories hits the physical base of AI growth directly.
