*Gold is gaining a job between nations. It is not coming back as the thing behind your dollars, and the math of revaluing it is smaller than the stories.*

If Set 1 is buying Set 2, what is left outside the dollar board? Two candidates: gold, which states hold, and Bitcoin, which mostly individuals hold. The project tested both against money that moved.

### Gold: the neutral corner

Central banks bought 863 tonnes of gold in 2025 and 289 tonnes in Q2 2026, buying into a price about 27% below its January high. Poland and China led. France brought 129 tonnes home. 89% of central banks surveyed expect official gold to rise. Round 5 update: the 2026 buyers are central banks without a standing Fed swap line, and 9% of central banks raised domestic storage in the past year, up from 5%. Ruling Db-04: gold as the neutral reserve layer, Confirmed, 75. The say and do gaps are real too: Russia sold 22 tonnes, Turkey sold, and the first half of 2026 (345 tonnes) was the weakest since 2022.

Why the buying? US secondary sanctions now reach dollar, digital and gold channels. Russia's case shows both sides: about €202 billion of its euro assets are still immobilized at Euroclear, while its gold held at home was never frozen. But Western markets banned its bars, so Russia now sells gold at home to fund its budget. The decoders grade "weaponized dollar rails push states into gold held at home" D1 (80).

> **Picture this.** Fire insurance does not need to be worth as much as your house. It needs to pay out in the one disaster you fear. Gold does not insure the world's debt, most of which is owed and settled in the borrower's own currency. It insures a state's ability to pay abroad when its dollar or euro reserves can be frozen. Against that job it is large: official gold is about $5 trillion against $13.2 trillion of allocated foreign exchange reserves, and at end 2025 prices it was 27% of official reserves, more than Treasuries at 22%.

> **Round 4 update.** Measured at market prices, gold is now about 26% of all official reserves (foreign currency plus gold), worth about 1.8 times all euro reserves. The ECB's own count for end 2025 put gold at 27% of global official reserves, against 22% for US Treasuries and 15% for the euro. Gold has quietly overtaken the euro as the world's second reserve asset. Because the dollar's lost share went to gold and to many smaller currencies rather than to one rival, the project now shows its neutral-layer scenario in two parts: gold and Bitcoin (13 to 2028, 17 to 2035, 18 to 2050) and a multipolar mix of currencies (2, 6 and 10). The totals are unchanged.

### Gold supply: not running out, not exploding

About 222,600 tonnes of gold exist above ground, worth about $29 trillion. Known reserves (about 61,000 tonnes) would add about 27% to that, and known resources (114,400 tonnes) about 51%. Mines produced a record 3,672 tonnes in 2025, so the stock grows about 1.5% to 1.7% a year and would be about 39% larger by 2050. Asteroids are no answer before then: metal asteroids hold about 1 gram of gold per tonne, so returning 1,000 tonnes would mean processing roughly a billion tonnes of asteroid metal (model). Asteroid gold breaking scarcity: D3.

### The revaluation math

The US holds 261,498,926 ounces of gold, booked at the old statutory price of $42.2222, so $11.04 billion. At the market price of $4,078, the one-off gain from marking it up would be about $1.06 trillion: roughly half of one year's deficit and about 2.7% of the debt. Here is what larger targets would need (model):

| Revaluation gain equal to | Gold price needed per ounce |
|---|---|
| 10% of debt held by the public | about $12,500 |
| All foreign official Treasuries | about $14,400 |
| All base money (cash plus reserves, $5.51T) | about $21,100 |
| All debt held by the public | about $124,600 |

So the popular $10,000 to $20,000 targets match "cover a slice", not "cover the debt". Precedents exist (1934: $20.67 to $35, 1972 to 1973: $38 then $42.22), but each came when gold still anchored the dollar by law. The gain would also be new base money, and with the Fed paying interest on reserves near the bill rate, swapping debt for reserves saves almost no interest. Odds from the judges: formal US revaluation by 2029-01-20 about 10, by 2035 about 15, an official price of $10,000 or more by 2035 about 3. Market gold at or above $10,000 by end 2030 about 20, at or above $20,000 by 2035 about 7.

### Why not 10x

The owner brought a belief to this project: gold of about $30 to $50 trillion times a 10x collateral multiplier equals the world's debt, and Bitcoin times 10 could support about $300 trillion. Round 1 audited it. The debt to gold ratio has wandered between 12x and 53x, so "gold times 10 equals debt" is a coincidence of one moment. The multiplier also contradicts the owner's own Bank of England citation: banks create money by lending, not by stacking credit on a gold base. Bitcoin times 10 would imply $1.43 million per coin, and real crypto lending runs at about 0.05x. Gold as "what money is backed by": D4.

*[Picture on the web page: The layers of money. Base money is a Fed liability backed mainly by $4.57 trillion of Treasuries. Bank deposits are created when banks lend. Stablecoins are backed by bills. Gold is a separate asset, not the base under any of them.]*

- **Myth:** Gold is what the dollar is really backed by.
- **Reality:** The legal gold cover behind Federal Reserve notes ended in 1968, and convertibility ended in 1971. In today's system bonds are what most money is backed by: the Fed's main asset is Treasuries, stablecoins hold bills, and Treasuries are claims on future taxes. Gold is the asset states hold in case that system is turned against them.

### Bitcoin: politically blessed, not a reserve

The US holds 198,000 to 328,000 forfeited bitcoin, has bought none, and the White House said on 2026-10-14 that purchases need Congress. The proposed ARMA law bars borrowing against, taxing or pledging federal assets and imposes a 20-year hold. US ETFs hold 1.29 million BTC and Tether 98,933. No G20 central bank holding is established. In 2026 Bitcoin rose while gold fell from May to September, fell in hawkish episodes, and traded in a range of $57.7k to $116k over a year. Ruling Db-05: in H0 Bitcoin is a risk asset with political status and a frozen sovereign stockpile, Confirmed, 80. As a future neutral reserve between nations: Speculative, 20 (by 2050).

> Bitcoin is energy-priced, not energy-backed (Da-07, Confirmed, 85). Its difficulty adjustment makes miners' energy spend follow the price, but you cannot redeem a coin for a kilowatt hour. The revealing move was by miners themselves: listed miners signed more than $70 billion of AI and data center contracts, because connected power near fiber now earns more as compute. Stranded remote power still earns most as Bitcoin, the only way to turn it into a globally movable asset.

One correction from the audit: the line often attributed to Putin, that Trump uses crypto to devalue US debt, came from adviser Anton Kobyakov on 2025-09-06 and was about stablecoins. As a devaluation plan it grades D3.

**Check yourself.** Q: A friend says gold must hit $20,000 to match the debt. What would you say?
A: No mechanism links gold to the debt. The ratio has wandered from 12x to 53x, gold fell 27% in 2026 while debt rose, and even a US revaluation to $21,100 would only match base money, not the debt. Gold can rise on central bank and investor demand, which is a different story.
