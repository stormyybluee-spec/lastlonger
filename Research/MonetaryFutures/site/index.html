Core question

Energy will not replace the dollar. It is replacing gold's old job.

What energy replaces. Until 1971 gold was the scarce physical thing that capped how much real output any money could claim. After 1971 the cap became political: how much debt the bond market would absorb. In an AI and robot economy, output scales with delivered electric power, and power cannot be printed. So energy becomes the real ceiling on what every dollar can buy. Gold's old job, done by watts.

How. Energy is entering the system as collateral, inside the dollar. Power plants, data center campuses, power purchase agreements and GPUs now back tens of billions of new dollar loans, and the first compute futures settle in dollars. Call it the watt-dollar, heir to the 1974 petrodollar. It strengthens whichever currency prices the watts. Today that is the dollar.

Why not as money you spend. A kilowatt-hour is perishable, tied to a place (PJM wholesale power rose 41% in 2026 while California hit record lows), and keeps getting cheaper as solar scales. A coin pegged to a kWh would lose value. Every energy currency since Henry Ford's 1921 proposal failed for these reasons.

Musk's version. On 2026-10-20 Musk said currency "will be measured in fundamental physics metrics like power, wattage and tonnage". He ties it to an economy "a million times" larger than today's, after robots and space. That is a post-scarcity accounting unit. It is close to the 1930s Technocracy "energy certificate" plan (his grandfather was a member). Its real problem is who sets the allocations.

Say versus do

Bessent criticized Yellen's bill-heavy issuance, then issued even more bills. His notepad said "$5-10 bil" for the yen operation. Treasury's own fund statement shows $500M.

Warsh argued for cuts as a candidate, then hiked as chair, and kept Fed money out of the yen trade.

Japan pledges alliance support while its Treasury holdings fell $171B from February to August.

The weak spot

The plan shortens the debt and ties federal interest to the Fed's rate, just as official foreign buyers leave and leveraged private buyers replace them. A lasting oil-driven inflation shock (Brent near $110, Hormuz still mostly closed) could force the Fed to cap yields. That is the path to the disorderly scenario.

Your "new monopoly board" question

Not a merger. An acquisition. The old system is buying the new rails.

You asked whether gold, bonds and credit (Set 1) and crypto, stablecoins and AI rails (Set 2) merge into a new board. The evidence says Set 2 is being absorbed into the dollar system rather than replacing it. Confidence 65.

The bank

The Federal Reserve. Fedwire stays final settlement. Crypto firms get limited payment accounts.

What backs it

Short Treasury bills, by law. Every compliant stablecoin dollar must buy them.

Who issues

Federally permitted issuers and tokenized bank deposits. A government CBDC is banned by statute until 2030-12-31.

The rails

Blockchains for retail and machine payments. AI agents pay in dollar stablecoins, not a new unit.

Toll booths

Issuers keep the float yield (holders get none by law). Custodians, compliance freeze points, Treasury's cheaper funding.

Neutral corner

Physical gold between nations, now worth more than euro reserves at market prices. Mostly a price effect.

Bitcoin

A frozen government stockpile and ETF asset (1.29M BTC). A favoured risk asset, not a reserve asset yet.

Rival boards

China chose the opposite: a public e-CNY and a stablecoin ban. The EU plans a digital euro around 2029.

Three standpoints

What it means for each seat at the table

Governance

  • The real lever is the primary deficit. Stabilizing debt needs about 2.9% of GDP of adjustment or 5.5% real growth.
  • Captive demand buys time, not solvency.
  • Energy policy is now monetary policy. Power capacity sets the ceiling on real output.
  • A cash UHI of $30k per adult is 24% of GDP. Deficit-financed, debt passes 200% of GDP within 5 years. Any UHI must be part ownership or offset.
  • Keep energy and compute priced in dollars, since China owns most solar hardware.

Business and investors

  • Firm power and grid connections are the scarce asset. PJM capacity cleared at its price cap.
  • AI gains are landing in margins: unit profits up 43% annualized, labor share at a record low 52.8%.
  • Stablecoin rails become dollar distribution channels from 2027.
  • Treasury supply stays heavy and short. Real yields carry the cost of capital, not inflation fear.
  • Food: commodity operators get squeezed, experience and human-made venues gain.

Wage earners and UHI recipients

  • Income stays in dollars through at least 2030. No energy or compute payments are planned.
  • The underclass risk is not losing credit. US law already makes lenders count steady government income.
  • The real gap is ownership. The bottom half of US households own 0.6% of stocks. Asset values follow the capital share, wages do not.
  • Rising power prices near data centers erode fixed incomes.
  • Be skeptical of "escape the permanent underclass" pitches. The phrase started as a tech meme and is used to sell products.
