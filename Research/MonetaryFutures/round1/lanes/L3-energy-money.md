## 0. Bottom line (one paragraph)

Energy is not becoming money you spend (a), and it is not becoming the official unit of account (c) in any horizon before H3. What the money trail shows (T0/T1/T3) is that energy is becoming (d) the binding real constraint of the AI economy and (b) a fast-growing collateral layer, and that this collateral is being financed, priced and settled in US dollars. Every energy-linked financial instrument found in 2025 to 2026 (corporate PPAs, data center campus bonds, GPU-backed loans, GPU-hour futures, GPU-collateralized stablecoin loans) is dollar-denominated. So the near-term pattern is a "watt-dollar" (energy and compute as the asset behind dollar credit), the direct descendant of the 1974 petrodollar, not a replacement of the dollar. Musk's "currency measured in wattage and tonnage" is coherent only as an H3 accounting unit for a post-labor economy, and it is a near copy of the 1930s Technocracy Inc. energy-certificate idea, a movement his own maternal grandfather belonged to. Energy fails the store-of-value test because its production cost keeps falling (solar learning rate about 20% per doubling) and it fails the medium-of-exchange test because it is perishable and location-bound (2026: PJM wholesale power up 41% while California hit historic lows). Its strongest monetary role is as the physical anchor that every money is ultimately a claim on.

### 1.2 Actor history: Musk's family link to Technocracy

Musk's maternal grandfather, Joshua N. Haldeman, was involved in Howard Scott's Technocracy Incorporated from 1936 to 1941 and was arrested by the RCMP on 1940-10-08 under the wartime ban on the organization (Wikipedia, citing The Leader-Post, 1940). Technocracy's central proposal was to replace the price system with energy certificates. Musk's 2026 phrase "currency measured in ... wattage and tonnage, mass and energy" is, in substance, the Technocracy program restated for an AI-and-robots economy. This does not prove intent, but it is exactly the "read the actor's own history" signal the Brief asks for: the idea is generational, not improvised.

### 1.3 History: every energy-money proposal so far, and why it failed

| Proposal | Year | Design | What happened | Why it failed (mechanism) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Henry Ford (with Edison), Muscle Shoals | Dec 1921 (New York Tribune, 1921-12-04) | Finance the Muscle Shoals dam with about $40M of new currency instead of bonds or gold. | Frederick Soddy, "Wealth, Virtual Wealth and Debt" | 1926 (2nd ed. 1933) | Real wealth obeys thermodynamics and decays. Money and debt ("virtual wealth") obey mathematics and compound forever. | Technocracy Inc. (Howard Scott, M. King Hubbert), Technocracy Study Course | 1933 to 1934 | Abolish the price system. Each citizen gets energy certificates equal to a share of national energy output. Certificates expire each period, are non-transferable, non-negotiable, non-interest-bearing, tied to ID. | Public interest peaked 1932 to 1933. Banned in Canada 1940 to 1943. Steady decline after 1948. Still exists as a small group. | Non-transferable, expiring certificates are rationing coupons, not money (no medium of exchange, no store of value). | Buckminster Fuller, Critical Path | 1981 | A world electric grid would make kilowatt-hours, not gold or dollars, the basis of economic accounting ("Kilowatt Dollar" label appears only in secondary sources). | No implementation. | Vision without mechanism. Depended on a global grid that would erase location-dependence. That grid does not exist. |
| Petrodollar (Simon, Parsky, Jeddah) | 1974 | Not energy money. Oil invoiced in dollars plus Saudi surpluses recycled secretly into Treasuries in exchange for US security and arms. | Worked for 50 years. Saudi holdings disclosed only in 2016 after a Bloomberg FOIA. | It succeeded precisely because it did not make energy the money. It made energy demand into dollar demand. This is the only energy-anchored monetary arrangement that ever scaled. |

Common failure pattern: (1) energy is not fungible across place, time, and form, so a "unit of energy" has no single price. (2) Energy money strips the state of seigniorage and fiscal flexibility, so states never adopt it. (3) Designs that make energy claims non-transferable kill the medium-of-exchange role. (4) The one success (petrodollar) used energy to anchor demand for an existing fiat money.

### 1.5 Bitcoin as "converted energy"

Claim (Musk 2025, Saylor's "digital energy"): Bitcoin is energy money because proof-of-work burns electricity.

Mechanism check:
- Causality runs from price to energy, not energy to price. Difficulty adjusts so that miners spend roughly as much energy as the block reward is worth. Energy sets a marginal cost floor that moves with price, it does not back the coin. BTC cannot be redeemed for kWh.
- So Bitcoin is energy-priced at the margin, not energy-backed. It is the purest existing "energy monetization" machine: it turns stranded or surplus power anywhere with an internet connection into a globally transferable bearer asset. That is a real feature, and it is the one thing kWh itself cannot do.
- Network use: Cambridge CBECI estimates were around 175 to 195 TWh in 2025 (peak about 194.8 TWh in Sept 2025), about 0.5% of world electricity. Hashrate peaked near 1,150 to 1,160 EH/s in Oct 2025.

The 2026 anomaly (T0/T3): the energy-to-money converters are leaving. CoinShares Q1 2026: hashprice fell to about $28 to $30 per PH/s per day (five-year low), weighted average cash cost per BTC among listed miners about $80,000 (Q4 2025), more than $70B of AI/HPC contracts signed by listed miners, some miners projected to earn up to 70% of revenue from AI by end-2026. IREN, TeraWulf, Core Scientific, Cipher, Hut 8 are becoming data center landlords. Bhutan, which built its BTC pile with hydro mining, has sold about 70% from its Oct 2024 peak (about 3,119 BTC left in May 2026, per Arkham) and seems to have stopped mining. Pakistan announced 2,000 MW for mining plus AI (May 2025), status unverified.

Revealed preference: the owners of power plus grid interconnects get a higher return converting electricity into compute than into Bitcoin. The market is voting that compute, not BTC, is the better "energy money" in H0. The default (miners mine) was broken.

### 1.7 Energy-backed tokens and kWh stablecoins

- WePower (2017 ICO): tokens representing 1 kWh of future production. Ran an Estonia grid pilot that put 24 TWh of data on Ethereum (39 billion "smart energy tokens"). Concluded fully decentralized large-scale use was not feasible. WPR trading now minimal.
- Power Ledger: "Sparkz" stable tokens for peer-to-peer kWh trades. Small. No scale data found.
- SolarCoin: no current status found.
- No kWh-denominated stablecoin of material size was found (as of 2026-10-22).
- What did scale is the reverse structure: dollar stablecoins lending against energy-and-compute assets (USD.AI).

Why kWh tokens fail: retail electricity is regulated and settled by grid operators, so the token adds nothing to the physical transfer. A kWh "delivered" in Texas is not a kWh in Estonia. Holders want dollars, not a claim on a resource whose price falls. The tokens became speculative coins detached from their kWh.

### 1.8 Academic proposals

- Sgouridis (Masdar), "Defusing the Energy Trap", Frontiers in Energy Research, 2014-02-26: distinguishes energy-backed currencies (redeemable, e.g. local energy credits, fit for community scale) from energy-referenced currencies (pegged to total energy supply, not redeemable, paired with a Chicago Plan separation of money and credit). Admits trade with non-adopters needs energy auditing and the transition would disrupt the loan cycle. This is the most rigorous version and it lands on (c) reference unit, not (a) spendable money.
- Ayres and Warr (2005, 2010): useful work (exergy) explains much of the growth that standard models leave to "technical progress". Supports (d): energy services are the binding input. They do not propose an exergy currency.
- Amado, "The Joule Standard" (MPRA working paper, 2025): claims money's value tracks the energy cost of labor over 10,000 years. Unreviewed, speculative.
- Soddy and Hubbert remain the intellectual roots.

The exergy point is the strongest objection to a joule standard: a joule of electricity, a joule of low-grade heat, and a joule of oil in the ground are worth very different amounts. Any energy unit has to specify form, place and time, which turns it back into a price index.

### 1.9 Mechanism test: energy vs the functions of money

Scored 1 (fails) to 5 (excellent). "Energy" here means a delivered kWh (or joule) of usable power.

| Function | Gold | Treasuries | Fiat credit (USD) | Energy (kWh) | Why energy scores this way |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium of exchange | 2 | 2 | 5 | 1 | Cannot be handed over without a grid. Perishable (storage costs real money and loses energy). Location-bound (2026: PJM +41% vs CAISO historic lows, negative prices in some hours). Every "energy payment" today is a dollar payment for energy. |
| Store of value | 4 | 3 (real yield, inflation and confiscation risk) | 2 | 1 | Supply is elastic and cost falls: solar modules fall about 20% per cumulative doubling (Swanson). China module price CNY 1.802/W (2021) to 0.683/W (2025). A unit whose production cost keeps falling loses purchasing power against goods. Gold works because supply is inelastic. Energy is the opposite. |
| Unit of account | 2 | 1 | 5 | 2 now, 3 in a machine economy | The joule is a fixed SI unit, which is appealing. But the economic value of a joule depends on form (exergy), place and time, so a usable unit needs a standard "delivered firm kWh at hub X", which is again a price index. GPU-hours are already used as a contract unit (CME 730 GPU-hour contract) but priced in USD. |
| Collateral / backing | 4 | 5 | n/a | 4 (as contracted cash flows) | PPAs, interconnects, campuses and GPUs already secure tens of billions of dollar debt. Weakness: GPU depreciation and power price swings. |
| Binding real constraint / ultimate claim | n/a | n/a | n/a | 5 | IEA, PJM at cap, turbine and transformer shortages. In an AI economy, output scales with energy and compute, so all money is increasingly a claim on energy-dependent output. |

Jevons check: efficiency gains do not remove the constraint. Nadella's argument and the IEA numbers (AI demand roughly tripling by 2030 despite efficiency) mean total energy demand rises as compute gets cheaper. That reinforces (d) and weakens (c): the price of a kWh relative to other things will not be stable while demand keeps jumping.

Falling-cost check: if energy is the reference unit and solar keeps getting cheaper, the energy unit appreciates in real goods terms only if demand outruns supply (Jevons), otherwise it depreciates. Either way it is unstable as a yardstick. This is the core reason energy fails as money, and the same reason it succeeds as the thing money buys.

## 3. Anomalies (deviation from default)

1. Miners abandon mining. Default: high-energy, cheap-power operators keep mining Bitcoin. Observed: >$70B in AI contracts and planned shutdown of mining sites. Intent revealed: energy's best monetization in H0 is compute, which is sold for dollars.
2. Musk endorses energy-money for Bitcoin in Oct to Nov 2025 after attacking Bitcoin's energy use in 2021. Default: a carmaker or rocket maker ignores monetary theory. Signal: narrative alignment with his energy and compute asset base and with the debasement trade.
3. Musk speaks of "200 GW a year" in the present tense. Default: describe a target as a target. Signal: the number anchors the "energy is the new money supply" story to his companies.
4. The money form of compute is the dollar. CME's first compute futures are cash-settled in USD. A truly new "compute currency" would have settled in compute. Default for a new commodity: futures settle in the dominant currency, which is what happened. Confirms dollar extension, not replacement.
5. GPU-collateralized loans funded by a dollar stablecoin (USD.AI). Default for "energy money" enthusiasts would be a kWh or compute token. What scaled is the opposite: a dollar token with energy-compute collateral.
6. Corporate PPA volume fell 10% in 2025 while AI power demand surged. Default: more AI demand means more PPAs. Explanation offered: policy uncertainty, negative prices, lack of mega-deals, plus a shift to on-site gas and nuclear deals. Signal that power is getting harder to contract, so the constraint is tightening (supports d).
7. US is a net energy exporter, so the petrodollar's recycling logic has inverted: the US is now the energy seller. A dollar-priced energy reference benefits the US directly.

## 4. Mechanism chains

Chain A (watt-dollar, H0 to H1, Plausible):
AI demand grows faster than grid supply (IEA, PJM) → firm power and interconnects become the scarce asset → hyperscalers and neoclouds borrow dollars against power-secured campuses, GPUs and PPAs → new dollar credit is created against energy assets (like mortgages against houses) → demand for dollar funding and dollar collateral rises globally → dollar-settled compute and power derivatives (CME, Kalshi) standardize the units → energy becomes the reference asset behind a growing share of dollar credit. Test: GPU and data center debt issuance, compute futures open interest, PJM and ERCOT capacity prices, data center ABS spreads.

Chain B (Musk's debt claim, H1 to H2, Speculative):
Robots plus AI lift real growth above the real interest rate → debt/GDP falls without austerity → but this needs energy supply to grow at the same pace as output, because AI output is energy-bound → if energy supply lags (turbine shortages, interconnect queues, permitting), the growth dividend shows up as higher power prices and inflation in energy-intensive goods instead of falling debt ratios → so the debt solution is really an energy-supply bet. Test: US electricity generation growth vs real GDP growth, PJM and ERCOT prices, CPI electricity component.

Chain C (China as the energy mint, H1 to H2, Plausible):
If value is measured in energy, the marginal source of new energy is solar plus batteries → China has about 1,100 GW of module, 1,400 GW of cell and 1,500 GW of wafer capacity (end-2025), many times global demand → an energy-referenced system would give China the role the US Treasury plays now (issuer of the marginal unit) → so the US has a strategic reason to keep energy priced in dollars and to onshore solar (Musk's 200 GW, tariffs) and to push gas, nuclear and LNG exports. Test: US cell capacity growth, Chinese export tax rebate removal (April 2026), US tariff and FEOC rules, LNG export volumes.

Chain D (Bitcoin energy floor, H0 to H1, Plausible):
BTC price falls or halves reward → hashprice falls below cash cost (about $80k per BTC in Q4 2025) → miners divert power to compute → hashrate falls, difficulty adjusts down → energy "backing" per coin falls → this proves energy follows price. Test: hashrate, difficulty adjustments, share of miner revenue from HPC.

Chain E (H3 Technocracy redux, Speculative):
Labor no longer scarce → prices of most goods fall toward energy + materials + compute cost → money's remaining job is rationing energy, mass and land → an allocation unit in joules or kg emerges → whoever sets allocations holds power (the old Technocracy problem) → likely owners: whoever owns generation, launch and compute. Test: none observable before 2035. Watch for official proposals to index transfers to energy or compute.

## 5. Incentive map
