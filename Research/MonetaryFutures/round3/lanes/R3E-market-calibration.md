## Do market odds suggest changing S1 to S4?

Short answer: keep the numbers (H0 62/15/1/22, H1 45/22/3/30, H2 32/28/10/30), and change what S4 rests on.

1. No contract prices the dominant regime at end 2028, so markets can only test S4's ingredients. The desk listed five for H0: the Hormuz oil shock, long yields above 5%, a shrinking official holder base, France at 157.5bp, and a bill-heavy debt that reprices with every hike.
2. Ingredients the markets support or strengthen. Hormuz stays disrupted (normal before 2028: about 48%, deep). A December hike is likely (75 to 79%). A recession starting in 2027 runs at 20 to 21%. The 10y reaching 5.5% before 2027 runs at 25%. France is likely downgraded, and the budget is a coin flip (thin). A Le Pen presidency is at 46%, which matters for the euro leg of S4 in H1.
3. Ingredients the markets weaken. Forced yield control by a captured Fed: Warsh out by mid-2027 9.5%, Cook out by year-end 8%, no cut priced before mid-2027, reserve purchases paused. An acute US bond accident before 2027: the 10y at 6% is 6.5%, and MOVE is falling. A French default is not priced in any market worth reading.
4. Net effect. The weaker Fed-capture route roughly offsets the stronger oil-inflation and euro routes, so S4 H0 stays at 22. In the Db-09 narrative, the likeliest S4 path in H0 should be relabeled from "forced Fed yield control" to "an oil-shock inflation and term-premium episode with the euro periphery as the weak link, met by a Fed that hikes rather than caps yields". That is closer to 1970s stagflation than to 1940s yield control, and it delays the repression episode (S4-lite) toward H1, where Fed turnover is priced (Powell's seat 64 to 72% vacated by early 2028).
5. S2 and S3. Markets give no reason to move them. Gold and Bitcoin price contracts say nothing about official tonnage, and no market prices a compute or energy unit.
