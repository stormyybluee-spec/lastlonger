## 1. Answers

### A1. What does Trump himself want the monetary system to be like?

**Answer.** Trump has run one economic worldview for almost 40 years. The US is a rich customer that allies cheat: they run surpluses, keep their currencies weak and let America pay for their defense. His paid newspaper letter of 1987-09-02 already said "It's time for us to end our vast deficits by making Japan, and others who can afford it, pay", complained that Japan had kept "a weak yen against a strong dollar", and ended "Tax these wealthy nations, not America". From that worldview come four wants that show up in both of his terms. (1) Foreigners pay: tariffs, allies paying for protection, $1M "gold card" visas, so domestic taxes can fall. (2) A cheaper dollar against surplus countries, while the dollar stays the world's only toll road. On 2026-01-27 he called a four-year dollar low "doing great". At the same time he banned a US CBDC and backed GENIUS so private dollar tokens built on Treasury bills spread worldwide. (3) Very low interest rates on demand, from a Fed that answers to him: he wanted rates "near 1% or lower" after the 2026-09-16 hike, and he is trying to remove Governor Cook. (4) An "owner society" instead of welfare: Trump Accounts for 70 million children, and state equity stakes (Intel 9.9%). Crypto fits as a toll road where his family owns a booth: the family's crypto ventures made an estimated $2.3B pretax from November 2024 to April 2026 (Reuters, 2026-06-09). He shows no intent for a gold standard, a Bitcoin standard, a planned crash or a debt "reset". He has bought zero Bitcoin, gold is still booked at $42.22 an ounce, and every self-inflicted market drop so far was followed by a retreat. In one line: he wants a landlord's monetary system, where the dollar is the rent everyone must pay, money is cheap for the landlord and the tenants (allies) cover the costs.

- **Status:** Plausible (strong) for intent. It rests on T2 words over four decades plus T1 actions (GENIUS, CBDC ban, tariff orders, Cook process) and T0 family money.
- **Confidence:** 75 that this is his actual intent. 35 that he gets most of it by 2029-01-20.
- **Horizon:** H0 (now to the end of his term, 2029-01-20). Rails built now (GENIUS from 2027-01-18) last into H1.
- **What he can actually get (outcome):** the rails, yes (GENIUS is law, the CBDC ban runs to 2030-12-31). A cheaper dollar, only in episodes (the dollar hit a four-year low in January 2026, then strengthened from August, EUR/USD 1.170 to 1.123). Cheap rates, no so far (Warsh hiked 12-0, the 10y is 5.13%, the Supreme Court kept Cook). Foreigners pay, partly (IEEPA tariffs struck down 6-3 on 2026-02-20, effective tariff rate down to about 6.7% to 7.4% by July 2026, refunds of up to about $175B owed). The binding constraints are the bond market and the courts, not Congress.
- **Three views.**
  - Governance: expect policy to be built through rules, appointments and private rails (GENIUS, eSLR, Trump Accounts) because those need no 60 Senate votes. Watch the Fed: it is the one institution standing between his "cheap money" want and the outcome.
  - Business: plan for a dollar that is weaker against Asian surplus currencies over time but dominant as a payment rail, for tariffs that change legal basis rather than disappear, and for rates set by Warsh and the bond market rather than by the President.
  - Ordinary person: the President wants cheaper borrowing and a cheaper dollar, which would raise import prices and asset prices. Today you face the opposite (mortgages above 7%, the Fed hiking). Owning assets (index funds, a Trump Account for a child) is the side his system rewards. Holding cash or stablecoins earns the issuer the yield, not you.

### A2. If there are "secret powers", who are they and what do they want?

**Answer.** No hidden single hand is needed to explain the evidence. The influence is mostly visible in money, appointments and rule text. Four blocs shape the monetary agenda. (1) The Treasury-market establishment (Bessent, the dealer banks on TBAC, the big banks) has the most real power: issuance, bank rules (eSLR), buybacks and the GENIUS reserve rule all serve its goal of captive demand for Treasury debt without accidents (R1 L1). (2) Crypto and venture donors (Fairshake super PAC with about $193M cash in January 2026, including $25M from Coinbase, $25M from Ripple and $24M from a16z, plus David Sacks as White House crypto and AI czar) want legal rails, no CBDC and light SEC rules. They got most of it, but lost the CLARITY Act (cloture failed 49-50 on 2026-09-15) and got no federal Bitcoin buying. (3) The Trump family business (World Liberty Financial and its USD1 stablecoin, the TRUMP memecoin, American Bitcoin, Trump Media's Bitcoin treasury) wants open rails for its tokens and no ethics limits. CLARITY failed partly over ethics language on the president's crypto income. (4) Foreign money buying access: the Abu Dhabi fund MGX used $2B of USD1 to invest in Binance, and Trump later pardoned Binance founder Changpeng Zhao (October 2025). The link is documented as a sequence, but causation is not proven. Peter Thiel's influence is real through JD Vance and a personnel network, but no document shows him writing monetary policy. "Wall Street runs it" is the best-supported version of the secret-powers idea, and it is not secret: it is in TBAC minutes and refunding statements.

- **Status:** Confirmed for the existence and size of influence by blocs 1 to 4 (T0 donations, T1 appointments and rules, T2 statements). Speculative for any coordinated hidden plan.
- **Confidence:** 70.
- **Horizon:** H0. The 2026-11-03 midterms and the January 2027 reintroduction of CLARITY are the next tests of bloc 2 and bloc 3.
- **Three views.**
  - Governance: the conflicts of interest are now part of how rules get written. The fight over ethics clauses decides whether crypto market-structure law passes at all.
  - Business: lobbying spend works in this administration (GENIUS, CBDC ban, SEC exemptions). Banks and crypto firms are both winning, at the expense of anyone who wanted a public digital dollar.
  - Ordinary person: the family tokens made the Trumps an estimated $2.3B while outside investors lost about $2.25B to $2.3B (Reuters). The safest part of the new system is the regulated, Treasury-backed part, not the politically connected tokens.

## 3. Key findings

1. **The worldview is old and stable.** The 1987 letter (1987-09-02, $94,801 for ads in the New York Times, Washington Post and Boston Globe) contains the whole 2025 program in seed form: allies "taking advantage", a weak yen, "make Japan, and others who can afford it, pay", "Tax these wealthy nations, not America" (DCA-01, DCA-02). The 2019 "bigger enemy" tweet (DCA-03) and the 2026 "1% or lower" post (DCA-07) show the same Fed view. Intent decoding is easy here because the actor repeats himself.
2. **"Weak but dominant" is the core design, and it resolves an apparent contradiction.** Trump wants a cheaper exchange rate and a dominant dollar. The administration separated the two: the exchange rate is pressed through partners (yen intervention, imbalance talk, tariffs), while dominance moves to private rails (GENIUS stablecoins, CBDC ban) that keep the world using dollar tokens backed by Treasury bills.
3. **The money moved is smaller than the words, and the gap is the tell.** "Strategic Bitcoin Reserve": zero purchases. "Whatever it takes" for Japan: $500M. "Fort Knox audit": no independent audit. Gold revaluation: book value unchanged. The things that actually moved money were tariffs, stablecoin law and family token sales.
4. **Family money is the clearest motive signal, and it points to rails, not reserves.** The family's earnings come from issuing and selling tokens (WLFI governance tokens, USD1 float interest, memecoin, mining equity), not from holding Bitcoin. That is a toll-booth model. It fits a policy that builds legal rails and resists ethics limits, not one that buys Bitcoin for the state.
5. **Trump's strongest want (cheap money) is the one he is losing.** His own Fed chair hiked 12-0 on 2026-09-16, the Supreme Court kept Cook (2026-06-29), the 10y is 5.13% and the 30y mortgage is above 7%. The next lever is the Cook removal process (hearing 2026-11-05).
6. **"Foreigners pay" took a legal hit but survived.** IEEPA tariffs were struck down 6-3 on 2026-02-20. The administration moved to a 10% Section 122 tariff (2026-02-24 to 2026-07-24), then Section 301 tariffs of 10% to 12.5%. The effective rate fell from a 2025 peak to about 7%. Revenue fell, but the policy tool stayed.
7. **The "secret powers" are mostly visible.** Fairshake's $193M, Sacks's role, Bessent's dealer-bank network and the MGX-USD1-Binance sequence are all documented. The genuinely hidden part is narrow: what was promised to whom (for example around the CZ pardon). That part stays unproven.
8. **The conspiratorial branches split cleanly.** Stablecoins as captive debt demand is D1 (the Treasury Secretary chose the 1863 analogy himself). Gold revaluation and "Bitcoin pays the debt" are D3 or D4 on current money flows. A planned crash is D3 and contradicts his stock-market-as-scorecard habit and the Trump Accounts design.

5. A Trump-appointed Fed chair hiked within four months, and Trump blamed the "hostile" Board rather than the chair. Default: a loyalist cuts, or the President attacks the chair.
