# DEBATE R17: the Court on chapter rb-future, "Paying for tomorrow"

Court ruling on VERIFY_R17.md (VF-01 to VF-100) and REDTEAM_R17.md (RT-01 to RT-14, with its voices, flattery check, depth notes and word audit). Read against round13/OWNER_R13.md, FRAME_R17.md, DRAFT_R17.md and lanes R17A and R17B. General education, not advice.

## 1. Summary
Counts (docket rows): 71 accept, 16 accept in part, 4 reject. The first accept row groups 31 Verifier points that matched and need no change. Section 3 holds 96 edits and 6 new blocks. Blocks 6, 23 and 55 and tables 24 and 54 must be deleted by hand.

Main path: by the Court's own count (kicker, headings, lists, tables, callouts, all outside go-deeper boxes) it goes from 3,498 to 3,635 words, a net of +137. On the draft's own basis of about 3,750, that is about 3,890, under the limit. The room came from moving the research table, the late-developer table, the scholars' debate and the launch cost numbers into go-deeper boxes, with a short version of each left on the main path, and from deleting block 6, which repeats block 48.

What changes most:
- Test 3 is fair to the reader now. The rate range is corrected to 4.6% to 6.5%, and the money growth window is named as 1994 to 1999. Railway shares broke in late 1845, before the 1847 rate rises. Perez is quoted word for word, together with her "massive episodes of credit creation". A new paragraph explains how created money could reach technology, through loans to buy shares or a central bank buying government bonds. It says plainly that the second road was not tested here.
- The Brontë scene stays a scene. It now tells the sourced misstated accounts, with part of the 10% dividends paid from the owners' own capital, and the small deposits that made a share a promise. The unsourced aunt, novels and "bought near the top" lines are gone.
- The rocket test sets the 396 million for rocket and capsule against a model that priced the rocket alone. It gives NASA's first lesson, that partners paid over half, and adds the later cargo and crew promises and the OneWeb rescue.
- Korea's inflation range is kept as one account, with the note that it began before the heavy industry push. The 1997 crisis gets its newer causes. "Largely created money" now applies to export loans only.
- Penang drops "little borrowing" and "wages and skills stayed". It tells the women's work with respect: young women just out of school, night work allowed by law, microscope work that could blur the eyes, and Grossman's title Bitter Wages. No nickname is printed. Taiwan gains Philips and its first company. Proton gains its years as the leader in home sales.

The four findings now read:
1. Cost first: holds, for these cases, which all won in the end. Not every start was costly.
2. Promises pay: holds, with a correction. In these cases the payers were largely shares and the state as buyer, and bank loans mattered in some countries.
3. Created money: holds only in part, and one road, government borrowing bought by a central bank, was not tested.
4. The rocket: holds in part. NASA paid 396 million for a rocket and a capsule, against a model of billions for the rocket alone, and other firms failed.
