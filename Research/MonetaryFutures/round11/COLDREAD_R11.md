## Ranked fixes

Each fix: block, exact old text (occurs once in the draft), new text.

1. Block [29]. Old:
OLD: Three parts of the question need fixing. America's share of official gold peaked at about 70% in 1948, not 75%. The rebuild took about five years, not fifty.
NEW: The question assumed three things that need fixing. It assumed America held 75% of the world's official gold, but the share peaked at about 70% in 1948. It assumed rebuilding takes fifty years, but it took about five.

2. Block [32]. Old:
OLD: Whether gold alone could have done it cannot be tested.
NEW: So gold did not do the rebuilding, and a shortage of gold did not stop it. Whether a world fully on gold could have done it cannot be tested.

3. Block [16]. Old (threshold):
OLD: It fails the second: its deficit before interest, about 3.6% of income in 2024, was too big for that gap. Watch both.
NEW: It fails the second. With debt a little over one year of income and growth about 2 points above the rate, the government can run a deficit before interest of only about 2% to 2.5% of income without debt growing against income (our arithmetic). The real figure was about 3.6% in 2024. So by the strict test today's answer is a warning, not a collapse. Watch both.

4. Block [16]. Old (wording):
OLD: The rate beat growth in only 5 or 6 of the 25 years from 2001, but in 24 of the 64 years from 1962.
NEW: The rate was above growth, which is the bad case, in only 5 or 6 of the 25 years from 2001, but in 24 of the 64 years from 1962.

5. Block [15]. Old:

NEW: Whether that can go on depends on two numbers: the interest rate against how fast the economy grows in dollars, price rises included, and the deficit before interest, which is spending minus taxes with the interest bill left out.
