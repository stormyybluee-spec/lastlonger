Headline result. The arithmetic is sound and most numbers match their sources. The chapter's problems are of four kinds. (1) A wrong fact: the US was not on a metal standard from 1866 to 1897. (2) Causal claims about 1945 to 1951 that the sources do not support and that clash with the timeline (the 1953 debt deal cannot have rebuilt Europe by 1949). (3) Stale or mixed-series numbers on the debt test, the hyperinflation count, the generation table and the 1996 price-index bias. (4) The credit-money toll is sized in dollars, while the hard-money toll is not sized at all.
