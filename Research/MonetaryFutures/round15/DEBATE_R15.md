## 1. Summary

Counts: 130 points ruled. Accept 111, accept in part 18, reject 1. Section 3 has 97 edits (79 on the main path, 18 in the go-deeper boxes) and 11 new blocks. Four old tables (26, 40, 45, 47) and one block (22) are deleted by hand.

What changes most:
1. The chapter no longer says that no account gives money a part. Palma and Rousseau and Sylla are named, credit is shown inside the rules and state rows, and Palma gets his own row.
2. Test 1 now names the war or upheaval behind each case, counts Holland's rise for the claim, and drops "cheapest credit in Europe", "three times as rich", "stopped for 150 years" and the 11% Chinese figure. Where two data sets disagree (Holland in 1650, England 1650 to 1700, British debt in 1815, English reading), both are given in plain words.
3. Test 3 adds short credit, country banks and the Ventura and Voth view of war debt, fixes the mill cost range and Indiana, and adds two narrative paragraphs: how a war bond worked and one season at a mill.
4. Test 4 replaces "slowly rising to 1650" with "flat, then a dip", shows the 1650 to 1700 jump in both data sets with the switch to Great Britain, and explains the loop and the marriage brake step by step. The explanations table gains marriage, money, coal and colonies, and Atlantic trade.
5. A short "Beyond Europe" section (Melaka, Ming silver, India) says plainly that the evidence is mostly European. The late developer line is now an idea for later chapters. Main path: 3,074 words by the Court's count (about 2,900 by the frame's) rises to about 4,200.

The four findings now read:
- Part 1: "Holds in a weaker form. Money helped, but was not enough alone." Earned by Test 1: floods of money came with stalls, but Holland grew on cheap credit first and Palma's evidence stands.
- Part 2: "Holds", with the surprise that schools for everyone came after the take-off and that no one link was enough alone.
- Part 3: "Holds in part." States and networks ran on long promises. Factories were built mostly from owners' funds and ran on short credit.
- Part 4: "Holds as a description. The cause of the escape is argued." The rebuilt claim no longer says money was "not the link most often missing" and no longer gives late developers to the reader as a finding.
