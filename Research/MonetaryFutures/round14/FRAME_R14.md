# Round 14 frame (2026-11-16): Level 3 deep chapter on 1971

Chapter: working id rb-1971, the first of the seven deep chapters (round13/SERIES_L3.md). Working title: "The weekend that cut the anchor: what 1971 really changed".
It deepens question 5 of rb-credit and tests two of the owner's new points (round13/OWNER_R13.md).

## Hypotheses to test (the owner's points, with the question-mark rule)
- H1 (point 2). Before 1971 credit rested on something limited, because money was tied to scarce gold. Test: how tightly did gold really bind credit before 1971? Gold cover, the US gold stock against foreign dollar claims, how fast bank credit grew under the gold link.
- H2 (point 2). After 1971 unlimited borrowing is available. Test: what limits remained (inflation, bond markets, the exchange rate, capital rules), and do they bind the United States differently from a country like Malaysia?
- H3 (point 2). The new system runs under stable, controlled governance. Test: what replaced gold as the anchor, when, and how stable it has been (the inflation of the 1970s, the debt crises of the 1980s, 1997 in Asia, 2008, 2021 to 2023).
- H4 (point 5). The world after 1971 is the main subject, and its purpose is to let productivity and technology advance. Test: growth, crises and inflation before and after, for rich countries and for late developers such as Korea, Malaysia and China.

## Lanes
- R14A, data: the numbers for H1 and H4.
- R14B, governance and limits: H2 and H3, both sides' best case, and sourced first-hand accounts of people who lived through 1971 to 1998.
