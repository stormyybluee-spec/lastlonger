| Test | Question | An expert answer must contain |
|---|---|---|
| Test 3 | Where does a stablecoin dollar come from and where does it go, floor by floor? | Deposit to issuer, issuer buys bills, who sold the bills, the effect on reserves, the Treasury account and deposits, offshore holders, what happens on redemption and in a run |
| Test 4 | Does a strong dollar export inflation, and to whom, by regime? | Import prices, commodity invoicing, dollar debt, the US energy offset, which countries absorb it, evidence from 2022 and 2026 |
| Test 5 | What actually limits US deficits: the debt ceiling, the bond market, the Fed or inflation? | Each limit's mechanism, when each has bound in history, the interest bill, auction tails and term premium, fiscal dominance, the 1951 Accord |
| Test 6 | When the Fed shrinks its balance sheet, whose money disappears? | QT by run-off, Treasury refinancing from nonbanks vs banks, reserves vs deposits vs reverse repo, 2022 to 2025 evidence |
| Test 7 | Does buying a Bitcoin-backed preferred move money out of the dollar system? | Three meters (dollars move only by trade, ownership and risk cross, market value changes with no dollar crossing), the bank-loan and bank-buyer creation cases with signs, repayment destroying deposits, the stablecoin parallel (compliant coins buy bills, offshore coins can hold Bitcoin and gold), what reverses in a bust |

Grading: pass when every item is present and correct with numbers, partial when at least half are, fail otherwise. Record the result, the date and the missing items in PLAN.md section 4.6.

| Test 8 | Do the three largest index managers control American companies? | Legal owner against beneficial owner (the funds' clients hold the economic interest). The managers' combined share of the average large US company and how it grew. They vote the shares, and how often they vote with management (Form N-PX). | Test 9 | Was there a secret deal in 1974 to put Gulf oil dollars into US Treasuries, and has it ended? | The 1974 US and Saudi arrangement after the oil embargo (Treasury Secretary Simon's trip), purchases outside the normal auctions, the holdings kept out of the country tables for about four decades and released in 2016 after a records request. No treaty text with an expiry date is known, so the viral 2024 "50-year deal expired" claim fails. What the public data shows about Gulf Treasury holdings since, the custody caveat, the dollar pegs that still require dollar reserves, the share of oil sold to Asia and settled in other currencies, each with a grade (documented, footprint, structural) |
