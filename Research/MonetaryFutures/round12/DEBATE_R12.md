## 1. Summary

Points ruled: 124 (93 VF, 19 RT points, 8 RT voices, 4 RT sections). Accept: 92. Accept in part: 30. Reject: 2. Edits: 99. New blocks: 1.

1. The epilogue now follows the chapter. Parts 2 and 3 are graded "holds in part" (part 2 to match block 12, part 3 because poverty is not proof that people could not save). The "first money system" line goes, because credit is 4,000 years old by the chapter's own table.
2. The rebuilt claim keeps only what was tested. "Output grows fastest with credit", "main ladder", "cash melts" and "nothing but judgment limits credit" are cut or reworded.
3. Causal glue is removed. The growth table, the debt and investment pair, the owner and renter gap and the 100% threshold are each marked as a pattern, not a proof. The threshold is now flagged as disputed.
4. State printing is kept apart from bank credit. Zimbabwe is named as a state printing to pay its bills while output fell.
5. Both sides of the law are fairer. "Nobody asked" and "insiders" are softened. The deposit insurance table gets Canada and the 61-country study. Spain becomes a warning, not a proof. One short Islamic finance block is added for Malaysian readers, with one sourced number.
